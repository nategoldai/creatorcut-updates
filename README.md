CreatorCut turns your raw footage into a finished, captioned video. You choose the look in Studio, and your own Claude Code or Codex chat does the editing on your Mac.

## What you need

- **A Mac with an Apple chip:** M1, M2, M3, M4 or newer. Intel Macs aren't supported.
- **macOS 14 Sonoma or newer:** 14 Sonoma, 15 Sequoia, 26 Tahoe or 27.
- **Free space:** at least 6 GB to install, plus about 2.5 GB for each video while it's being edited.
- **An internet connection.**
- **Your own paid Claude Code or Codex subscription.** This isn't included.
  - Claude Code is the **Code** tab in the Claude app.
  - The normal Claude chat can't run CreatorCut.
- **The CreatorCut folder on your Mac's own drive.** Don't install it in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive or on a USB drive.

To check your Mac, click the Apple menu → **About This Mac**. The chip must say "Apple M…".

## Install

1. Open **Claude Code** (the Code tab in the Claude app) or **Codex**.
2. Paste this: **Read https://github.com/nategoldai/creatorcut-updates and install CreatorCut using install.sh.**
3. Wait until setup finishes. Finder then opens your new **CreatorCut** folder.

## Open Studio

- **Claude:**
  1. Click **New session**.
  2. Click the folder name under the message box → **Open folder…** → choose **CreatorCut** in your Home folder → **Open**.
  3. Click **Trust**.
  4. Type **open Studio**.
- **Codex:**
  1. Click **New chat** and choose the **CreatorCut** folder.
  2. Turn on **Full access**.
  3. Type **open Studio**.

## Make a video

1. Add your footage. B-roll and a reference video or link (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) are optional.
2. Choose captions, colour, music and up to two effects.
3. Press **Make my video**.
   - In Claude, it starts by itself. If nothing happens within 30 seconds, type **continue** in the chat.
   - In Codex, type **continue**.
4. Keep the chat open and your Mac plugged in and awake. If your chat asks a question, answer it there.
5. Watch and **Download** your video. To change something, add a note on the timeline and press **Update video**.
6. **Save as preset** keeps your style for next time.

A video usually takes 20–45 minutes. Your footage and earlier versions stay saved.

## Good to know

- **Footage:** built for vertical talking-head videos (one person speaking to camera).
- **Captions:** English and other Latin-alphabet languages.
- **Effects** are used only when they suit your clip. If one doesn't fit, your chat tells you why.
- **Library music** is Creative Commons. Include the credit Studio shows (**Copy credit**) when you post.
- **Privacy:** CreatorCut automatically sends install, update and editing status and technical problem reports. If you provide your purchase email, CreatorCut can link your activity to it. Your footage, audio, transcripts and files stay on your Mac.
